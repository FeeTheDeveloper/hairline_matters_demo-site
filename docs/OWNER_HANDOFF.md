# Owner handoff — Hairline Matters concept

**For:** King Fee, then the Hairline Matters owner during the pitch.
**Status:** private concept, not for redistribution. Not approved by the shop.
**URL:** `https://ftdsitepreviews.com/hairline_matters_demo/` (noindex; anyone with the link can view it). The Google Street View exterior was cut before deploy; the walk-in starts at the glass doors.

## What the shop gets

Modeled on the Oct 27 reference site, built from Hairline Matters' own material:

1. **Walk-in (scroll-driven):** scrolling moves the visitor through the glass doors and into the chair (locs, waves, curly taper). Headlines change with each beat and end on "Book your chair." A Skip intro button is always available. With reduced motion turned on, it falls back to a still and stacked text.
2. **At work:** full-width loop of the shop's own black-and-white film. It plays only while on screen and has a pause button.
3. **More than a cut:** short shop story, with a still from the film.
4. **Services:** links to the real booking page until prices are validated.
5. **Fresh out the chair:** a sideways-scrolling roll of six real client cuts, cleaned up but not altered.
6. **The space:** entrance and stations.
7. **Policies**, **Wear the line** (the #HairlineMatters tee), **Find the shop**, and a closing "Your chair is waiting" call to action.

The HM monogram is redrawn as a crisp vector for the header and footer.

## What we need from the owner before anything goes public

1. **Location:** exact address and suite. Sources disagree (Duncanville vs. Lancaster), so neither is published.
2. **Signage:** the storefront and mat read "FADED". Confirm how that relates to Hairline Matters.
3. **Exterior footage:** a 5–10 second phone walk from the parking lot to the door, to add back the arrival beat.
4. **Client consent:** confirm the clients in the six portfolio cuts and the walk-in clips agreed to appear.
5. **Services and prices**, **policies**, **hours and contact**.
6. **Shop story and numbers** (years open, etc.) for "More than a cut".
7. **Brand:** the original logo file and font, and the exact green (or approve `#2fd47a`).
8. **Tee:** price, sizes, and where it's sold.
9. **Go-ahead:** written approval to publish.
